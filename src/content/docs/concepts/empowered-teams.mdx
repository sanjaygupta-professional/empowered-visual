# Empowered Teams

## Concept Overview

The central concept in EMPOWERED is the distinction between **empowered teams** (missionaries) and **feature teams** (mercenaries). This isn't just about autonomy—it's about purpose, accountability, and trust.

```mermaid
mindmap
  root((Empowered Teams))
    Definition
      Given problems to solve
      Accountable for outcomes
      Discover their own solutions
    Characteristics
      High trust
      High autonomy
      High accountability
    Enablers
      Strong leadership
      Clear strategic context
      Coaching culture
    Outcomes
      Innovation
      Ownership
      Results
```
