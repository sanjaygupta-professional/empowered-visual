# Leadership

## Concept Overview

Leadership in the EMPOWERED context has two components: **leadership** (inspiration) and **management** (execution). Both are essential.

```mermaid
mindmap
  root((Leadership))
    Leadership Role
      Product vision
      Product strategy
      Product principles
      Team objectives
    Management Role
      Staffing
      Coaching
      Team topology
      Business collaboration
    Qualities
      Creates environment for greatness
      Develops people
      Trusts teams
```
