# Team Topology

## Concept Overview

Team topology is about structuring product teams for ownership, autonomy, and alignment.

```mermaid
mindmap
  root((Team Topology))
    Principles
      Ownership
      Autonomy
      Alignment
    Team Types
      Platform teams
      Experience teams
    Considerations
      Dependencies
      Proximity
      Evolution
```

## The Three Pillars

```mermaid
flowchart TB
    subgraph Pillars["TOPOLOGY PILLARS"]
        Ownership["OWNERSHIP<br/>Clear areas of responsibility"]
        Autonomy["AUTONOMY<br/>Ability to act independently"]
        Alignment["ALIGNMENT<br/>Connected to strategy"]
    end

    Ownership --> Empowered["Empowered<br/>Teams"]
    Autonomy --> Empowered
    Alignment --> Empowered

    style Pillars fill:#c8e6c9
```

## Platform vs Experience Teams

```mermaid
flowchart TB
    subgraph Platform["PLATFORM TEAMS"]
        P1["Enable other teams"]
        P2["Provide shared services"]
        P3["Technical focus"]
        P4["Internal customers"]
    end

    subgraph Experience["EXPERIENCE TEAMS"]
        E1["Own customer journeys"]
        E2["Drive business outcomes"]
        E3["Customer focus"]
        E4["External customers"]
    end

    Platform -.->|"Enable"| Experience

    style Platform fill:#e3f2fd
    style Experience fill:#c8e6c9
```
